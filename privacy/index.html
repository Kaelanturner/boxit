📦 BoxIt Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

The short version: your data is yours. It lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud — never on our servers, because there aren't any.

What BoxIt stores

Your moves, rooms, boxes, item lists, and box photos are stored on your iPhone. When iCloud is on, they also sync to the private iCloud database tied to your Apple Account, so your other devices see the same moves. That copy is encrypted by Apple and readable only by you. BoxIt's developer has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs, and cannot see anything you enter.

Your profile

Your profile name and photo stay on this iPhone only. They're part of your normal device backup but are not synced between devices.

On-device intelligence

When you scan a box's contents with the camera, the photo is analyzed by Apple Intelligence entirely on your device. BoxIt never uploads photos or item lists anywhere.

Permissions BoxIt asks for

  • Camera — to scan QR labels and photograph box contents.
  • Location — only when you tap Show My Location, to put a blue dot on the globe. Your position is never stored or shared.
  • Calendar — to add your moving dates as an event, and full access only to update or remove the event BoxIt itself created.
  • Notifications — for the moving-day reminders you choose.

Every permission is optional; BoxIt works without them.

Widgets and Live Activities

The Home Screen countdown and the moving-day Live Activity show your move's progress using data that stays on this iPhone.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple. BoxIt never sees your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

What you share is up to you

Labels, manifests, room signs, backups, and “Send Move” files leave your device only when you share them yourself through the iOS share sheet.

Deleting your data

Delete a move in BoxIt and it's removed from this iPhone and, through iCloud, from your other devices. To remove everything, delete your moves (or the app) and then delete BoxIt's iCloud data under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage. Export a backup from Settings › Your Data first if you want a copy.

Turning sync off

Switch BoxIt off under Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Apps Using iCloud. Export a backup first: iOS may clear the local copy when the iCloud account it was syncing with changes.

Children

BoxIt isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted here with a new date.
