📦 BoxIt Support

Questions, bugs, or ideas? Email kaelanturner@icloud.com — you'll hear back from the actual developer, usually within a day or two. In the app, Settings → Contact Support fills in your version and device for you.

Requirements

BoxIt runs on iPhone with iOS 27 or later. AI box scanning (point the camera at an open box to list what's inside) needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence; everything else works on every supported iPhone.

My QR label won't scan

Labels print best at 100% scale ("Actual Size" in the print dialog). If a label is taped over a seam or scuffed, open BoxIt → Scan and hold the camera 6–10 inches away; the code has built-in damage tolerance.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and synced privately through your own iCloud. Nothing is ever sent to us — see the privacy policy. You can export a full backup anytime from Settings → Your Data.

Sync isn't working

Check that both iPhones are signed in to the same Apple Account, that BoxIt is switched on under Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud, that iCloud storage isn't full, and that both are on iOS 27. Opening BoxIt on each device nudges the sync along.

Restoring a backup

Settings → Your Data → Import Backup, then pick the backup file. Moves already on the device are skipped, so importing twice is harmless, and printed labels keep working after a restore.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase, billed by Apple. To change or cancel a subscription, open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions (or Settings → Manage Subscription in BoxIt). Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Settings → Restore Purchases signs your Premium unlock back in on a new device.

Deleting your data

Deleting a move in BoxIt removes it from your iPhone and from iCloud. To remove everything, delete your moves (or the app), then delete BoxIt's iCloud data under Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.
